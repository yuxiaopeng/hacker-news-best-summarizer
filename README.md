在一个令人鼓舞的进展中，三名晚期胰腺癌患者在将其改良的溶瘤病毒ViraFect直接注射到肿瘤中后，表现出显著的疾病稳定或消退。胰腺癌以其恶性程度高、生存率低而臭名昭著，这使得这些初步发现意义重大。

## 9. Frostbite: A new CPU architecture discovered, designed and verified by AI

**原文标题**: Frostbite: A new CPU architecture discovered, designed and verified by AI

**原文链接**: [https://architectlabs.com/blog/frostbite](https://architectlabs.com/blog/frostbite)

The article introduces Frostbite, a novel general-purpose CPU architecture autonomously designed and verified by AI, demonstrating a radical departure from human-made designs. Frostbite leverages interaction nets, a computational model where programs are graphs with explicit, local dependencies and single-owner values. This approach inherently exposes parallelism, eliminating the need for traditional multithreading mechanisms, shared mutable state, and complex cache coherence protocols found in conventional CPUs.

On 25 diverse workloads, Frostbite achieved a 4.59x performance-per-watt multiple compared to a modern superscalar CPU core. The AI systems iterated autonomously across the entire design stack, including compiler, ISA, architecture, microarchitecture, RTL, verification, and GDS. A critical enabler was ASL, a new formal, machine-checkable specification language that served as the "source of truth," enabling tight iteration and preventing errors across the stack.

The AI discovered novel architectural primitives, including a dynamic runtime for interaction nets and an emergent work-stealing mechanism that distributes parallelism across tiles without a central scheduler. This project highlights AI's capability to push hardware design frontiers, suggesting that AI systems will fundamentally transform the architecture of all future chips.

## 10. 如果你无法简单地解释一件事，那说明你没有真正理解它。(2017)

**原文标题**: If you can't explain something in simple terms, you don't understand it (2017)

**原文链接**: [https://kottke.org/17/06/if-you-cant-explain-something-in-simple-terms-you-dont-understand-it](https://kottke.org/17/06/if-you-cant-explain-something-in-simple-terms-you-dont-understand-it)

The article "If you can't explain something in simple terms, you don't understand it" emphasizes that the ability to simplify complex ideas is a true measure of comprehension. It highlights physicist Richard Feynman, a Nobel laureate celebrated for his teaching prowess and commitment to explaining profound concepts to beginners.

A key anecdote reveals Feynman's intellectual humility: he admitted he couldn't explain why spin-one-half particles obey Fermi-Dirac statistics at a freshman level, concluding, "That means we don’t really understand it." This philosophy aligns with Apple's approach, where engineers are expected to explain complex technologies simply as proof of their complete understanding.

Feynman was renowned for his accessible explanations of topics like fire, rubber bands, and magnets. Crucially, he was also candid about the limits of his, or even collective scientific, understanding. He famously stated, "nobody understands quantum mechanics," despite winning a Nobel Prize for his contributions to the field. His rigorous standard for understanding was encapsulated in his blackboard note: "What I cannot create, I do not understand."
