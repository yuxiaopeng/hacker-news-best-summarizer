**原文链接**: [https://www.notebookcheck.net/LG-smart-TVs-caught-logging-audio-with-screen-off-and-snooping-on-local-devices.1391214.0.html](https://www.notebookcheck.net/LG-smart-TVs-caught-logging-audio-with-screen-off-and-snooping-on-local-devices.1391214.0.html)

由 Gamers Nexus、Level1Techs 和安全研究人员发起的一项新调查显示，LG 智能电视正在广泛记录音频并扫描本地网络，即使它们的屏幕看似关闭。对市售 LG OLED 型号进行的测试表明，这些电视会主动扫描家庭网络以寻找手机和智能手表等辅助设备，收集内部 IP 地址、邻近 Wi-Fi 网络详情（名称、信号强度）以及位置数据。

此次调查还揭示了 webOS 中存在的远程代码执行漏洞。鉴于这些普遍的数据收集行为和安全缺陷，研究团队建议将 LG 智能电视与互联网断开连接，并改用外部流媒体设备。LG 尚未对这些说法作出回应。

## 9. There's a new "Google Jail" for independent wikis

**原文标题**: There's a new "Google Jail" for independent wikis

**原文链接**: [https://weirdgloop.org/blog/google-jail](https://weirdgloop.org/blog/google-jail)

Independent wiki host Weird Gloop has identified a new "Google Jail" impacting brand-new domains since Google's March 2024 core update. This change means new websites, especially wikis launched on new root domains, typically only have their main page indexed by Google Search. Internal pages, which drive the vast majority of wiki traffic (85% for gaming wikis), remain undiscoverable, a catastrophic outcome for these projects.

This phenomenon has affected approximately 90% of new wikis launched since March 2024, lasting for an unclear period, sometimes up to a year. It's not a duplicate content or ranking issue for the main page, but rather a blanket failure to index internal pages. The author speculates Google might be aggressively combating SEO spam by effectively "nerfing" all new domains.

In contrast, wikis launched on subdomains of existing, established domains (e.g., overwatch.weirdgloop.org) are immediately and fully indexed. This has forced a strategic shift for independent wiki support. Rather than launching on desired root domains (e.g., overwatch.wiki), Weird Gloop is now temporarily hosting wikis on subdomains of their own established domain (weirdgloop.org), despite aesthetic concerns. The hope is to build Google authority on these subdomains, then later redirect them to their preferred root domains, retaining the accumulated "Google juice." The author is seeking further data and insights into this new domain authority issue.
