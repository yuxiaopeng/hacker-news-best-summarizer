## 4. 受欢迎的应用泄露了护照信息和超过20万条私信

**原文标题**: Lovable apps expose passport details and more than 200K private messages

**原文链接**: [https://www.crissyfield.de/research/lovable-en/](https://www.crissyfield.de/research/lovable-en/)

一份研究报告披露，许多基于瑞典人工智能平台“Lovable”构建的应用程序（该平台专为非程序员设计）会泄露敏感的个人和机密数据。Lovable允许用户通过自然语言描述来创建网络应用程序，但尽管其宣传的重点是易用性，其《条款与条件》却将安全责任归咎于客户，从而造成了一个“令人不安的漏洞”。

研究人员分析了从2025年12月到2026年9月期间的159,041个Lovable项目，重点关注了Supabase数据库集成。在34,545个使用Supabase的项目中，有24,664个最初在未要求登录的情况下发送了数据库记录。进一步的只读请求揭示了普遍的数据暴露问题。

重大数据泄露包括：
*   来自一个德国生活方式论坛的80,000多份用户资料（姓名、电子邮件、出生日期、性别）和200,000条聊天消息。
*   数万条门票和食品销售记录，包括电子邮件地址、电话号码、邮政地址和支付信息。
*   包含租户、业主和业务合作伙伴敏感细节的内部电子邮件的AI生成摘要。
*   **最关键的是，一家服务提供商泄露了1,000多份身份文件记录，包括护照号码、有效期和国籍，这带来了极高的身份盗窃风险。**
*   其他泄露内容包括合同、发票、个人相册、内部WhatsApp通讯以及280,000封客户电子邮件。

这些漏洞可能助长网络钓鱼、票务欺诈或身份盗窃。报告强调，Lovable重复出现的数据库集成模式使得攻击者更容易在多个应用程序中利用类似的错误配置。报告指出，易于使用的平台并不能消除在处理机密客户或业务数据之前进行专家安全审查和专业访问权限评估的必要性。自动化检查不足以确保安全性。

## 8. Court denies motion to dismiss DRAM price fixing case

**原文标题**: Court denies motion to dismiss DRAM price fixing case

**原文链接**: [https://litnews.ai/articles/garciaguirre-samsung-sk-hynix-micron-dram-conspiracy-suit-survives-dismissal](https://litnews.ai/articles/garciaguirre-samsung-sk-hynix-micron-dram-conspiracy-suit-survives-dismissal)

A U.S. District Judge Noël Wise has denied a joint motion by Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron to dismiss a proposed class action alleging they conspired to restrict the supply of conventional DRAM (like DDR5) and shift production to High Bandwidth Memory (HBM), thereby inflating prices. The judge found the complaint plausibly alleged a conspiracy in violation of the Sherman Act, distinguishing it from a prior dismissed case, DRAM II.

Plaintiffs, a group of consumers and small businesses, accuse the companies of cutting conventional DRAM supply since late 2022, leading to a 171.8% price increase by Q3 2025, despite the lack of expansion in commodity DRAM production.

Judge Wise identified several "plus factors" supporting a plausible inference of conspiracy. These included the companies' parallel actions in cutting supply and pivoting to HBM, coupled with "actions against self-interest" that would be perilous without prior agreement. Specific examples cited were long-term HBM supply lock-ups, refusals of long-term conventional DRAM contracts by Samsung and SK Hynix, Micron's closure of its Crucial brand, delays or halts in new DRAM plant construction, failure to expand despite record prices, and near-identical customer vetting practices adopted in January 2026. The judge also considered the companies' prior history of a criminal DRAM price-fixing cartel from 1998-2002.

However, the court gave no weight to arguments based on vague investor statements, unspecific executive meetings at industry conferences, or the market's oligopolistic structure.

The companies are ordered to answer by December 7, 2026, with the initial case management conference scheduled for January 12, 2027.
