## 2. Cloudflare 收购 Deno

**原文标题**: Cloudflare acquires Deno

**原文链接**: [https://deno.com/blog/cloudflare](https://deno.com/blog/cloudflare)

2026年10月9日，Ryan Dahl宣布Cloudflare已收购Deno，Deno整个团队将加入Cloudflare。这一战略举措旨在加速实现简化服务器软件开发的愿景，该愿景基于Deno在运行时、模块分发、安全性及应用程序分发方面的工作。

Dahl解释说，从Deno运行时到Deno Deploy，再到后来的`celld`——一个受Cloudflare Workers启发的分布式应用框架——这一发展历程与Cloudflare的目标完美契合。此次收购将Deno的成果与Cloudflare的Workers和Durable Objects团队相结合，力求将他们共同的编程模型确立为服务器开发的默认标准。

此次整合将为Deno用户带来重大变化：
*   Deno运行时将在未来一年内继续接收每月错误修复和安全更新，之后其开发将停止，但仍会保持开源。
*   Deno Deploy将继续运营六个月，然后关闭，届时将为付费客户提供迁移至Cloudflare Workers的支持。
*   JavaScript注册表JSR将继续运营，其基础设施将过渡到Cloudflare。
*   `rusty_v8`将继续获得支持并集成到`workerd`中。

Dahl对利用Durable Objects赋能AI表示兴奋，特别是对于那些需要廉价、无服务器执行、持久状态和WebSockets的代理框架。他和Kenton Varda在Cloudflare博客上提供了更多详情。

## 5. REA Reverse – Engineer Anything

**原文标题**: REA Reverse – Engineer Anything

**原文链接**: [https://rea.tools/](https://rea.tools/)

REA (Reverse – Engineer Anything) is a tool that empowers coding agents to inspect software and understand its inner workings. It streamlines the reverse engineering process, enabling agents to explain, modify, or rebuild software features.

Traditionally a manual effort, REA allows users to simply prompt their agent in plain English to investigate program behavior. For example, it can analyze why Windows Calculator produces "220" for "200 + 10%", revealing that the "%" button calculates a percentage of the *first* number after addition. REA provides the agent with instructions, decompiled code, and call details, which the agent then uses to explain the logic.

The tool demonstrates its capabilities with several examples:
1.  **Chrome's Dinosaur game:** REA can extract the game's acceleration rules, allowing an agent to rebuild a version with adjustable speed.
2.  **Windows Calculator's % button:** It can distinguish how the "%" button operates differently after addition (percentage of the first number) versus multiplication (converts current number to a decimal), enabling the agent to reconstruct a calculator handling both scenarios.

REA supports reverse engineering native binaries, JavaScript/Electron applications, and browser/runtime activity. Installation is via a simple `npx` command. Beyond analysis, REA facilitates ambitious projects like cloning websites or reconstructing game logic from executables, providing comprehensive guides and community support.

## 8. 2026年诺贝尔和平奖授予纳瓦尼特姆·皮莱

**原文标题**: Nobel Peace Prize for 2026 to Navanethem Pillay

**原文链接**: [https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/peace/2026/press-release/](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/peace/2026/press-release/)

The Norwegian Nobel Committee has awarded the Nobel Peace Prize for 2026 to Navanethem "Navi" Pillay, recognizing her exceptional courage and integrity in advancing a comprehensive global legal order. The prize honors her instrumental role in ensuring the prosecution of war crimes, crimes against humanity, and genocide, and her unwavering commitment to peace and international law.

Pillay's remarkable career began in apartheid South Africa, where she defended Nelson Mandela. She served as a judge on the High Court in South Africa, the International Criminal Tribunal for Rwanda (ICTR), and the International Criminal Court (ICC). She also held the prestigious position of United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights and chaired the UN Independent International Commission of Inquiry on the Occupied Palestinian Territory. Currently, she is a judge on the International Court of Justice.

Her contributions have significantly broadened the scope and impact of international law. Notably, at the ICTR, she helped establish that rape and sexual violence could constitute crimes against humanity and genocide, and that spreading propaganda could be considered incitement to genocide. The Nobel Committee underscored the enduring importance of resolving conflicts through international law, a principle integral to the prize's history, especially amidst current global conflicts and challenges to the rules-based order. They stressed that international law is an absolute necessity for peace and stability, and that justice underpins peace. Pillay's work, strengthening fraternity between nations and advancing tribunals for justice, embodies Alfred Nobel's vision, making her a beacon of hope for a more peaceful world.
