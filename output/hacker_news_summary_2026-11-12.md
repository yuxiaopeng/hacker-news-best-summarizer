## 2. 计算机图形学知识大全

**原文标题**: The Computer Graphics Book of Knowledge

**原文链接**: [https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~ph/nyit/masson/history.htm](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~ph/nyit/masson/history.htm)

《计算机图形学知识宝典》记录了计算机图形学（CG）从最初作为科学和工程可视化工具，到发展成为一种独特的艺术形式和工业应用的广泛历史。

20世纪50年代，计算机图形学从贝尔实验室和波音公司等政府和企业研究中心兴起，随后扩展到麻省理工学院和犹他大学等高校。20世纪40年代出现了早期的“计算机辅助”图形，值得一提的是，麻省理工学院的穆恩（Moon）教授和斯宾塞（Spencer）教授通过数学模型和物理纸张样本，制作出了第一个辐射度图像。20世纪50年代，艺术家约翰·惠特尼（John Whitney Sr.）利用模拟机制，贝尔实验室的先驱们创造了早期的计算机辅助视觉效果，比尔·费特（Bill Fetter）在波音公司试验了计算机辅助设计（CAD），这些都带来了进一步的进步。重要的创新包括示波器艺术、矢量示波器显示器、第一支光笔，以及通用汽车（GM）和IBM公司开创性的DAC-1系统。

20世纪60年代，计算机图形学取得了飞速发展。它成为一种艺术形式，惠特尼（Whitney Sr.）和贝尔实验室的艺术家们制作了早期的动画电影。来自ARPA（高级研究项目局）的关键资金（曾由伊凡·萨瑟兰（Ivan Sutherland）领导）促进了创新。重大突破包括解决了隐藏线问题（纽约大学的赫布·弗里曼（Herb Freeman））以及在麻省理工学院开发了URBAN5等CAD系统（尼古拉斯·尼葛洛庞帝（Nicholas Negroponte））。威廉·费特（William Fetter）于1960年创造了“计算机图形学”一词。戴夫·C·埃文斯博士（Dr. Dave C. Evans）因其在学术领域的领导力被誉为“奠基人”。这一十年还出现了第一款流行的电脑游戏“太空大战”（Spacewar），以及伊凡·萨瑟兰（Ivan Sutherland）1962年具有里程碑意义的“画板”（Sketchpad）论文，该论文引入了交互式图形通信，为现代计算机图形学奠定了基础原则。

## 4. 面向Mamba的注意力：跨架构蒸馏的秘诀

**原文标题**: Attention to Mamba: A Recipe for Cross-Architecture Distillation

**原文链接**: [https://machinelearning.apple.com/research/attention-mamba-distillation](https://machinelearning.apple.com/research/attention-mamba-distillation)

题为《从Attention到Mamba：跨架构蒸馏的秘诀》的论文提出了一种新颖的方法，用于将现有Transformer模型的知识蒸馏到类Mamba架构中。Mamba作为一种状态空间模型（SSM），与基于注意力的Transformer相比，以其更低的内存消耗和更高的吞吐量而闻名。

此前将Transformer跨架构蒸馏到Mamba的尝试常常未能保持教师模型（teacher）的性能，频繁需要混合Attention-SSM解决方案。作者们认为，Mamba的*有原则初始化*对于在不使用混合模块的情况下成功蒸馏至关重要。

他们提出了一种两阶段方法：
1.  利用核技巧的改编版本，将知识从传统Transformer蒸馏到一个线性化版本的Attention模型中。
2.  将此线性化的Attention模型蒸馏到一个经过适应的Mamba模型中，该模型*不含任何Attention模块*。

该方法使得蒸馏后的Mamba模型实现了14.01的困惑度（perplexity），与Pythia-1B Transformer教师模型的13.86非常接近。这一性能在下游任务中大体上得以延续，尽管在依赖于上下文回忆（in-context recall）的任务上出现了一些差距。通过广泛的消融实验（ablations）、缩放分析（scaling analysis）和敏感性分析（sensitivity analysis），验证了该方法的有效性。

## 5. AI用户工作日时长比非用户多出长达90分钟。

**原文标题**: AI Users have workdays up to 90 min longer than non-users

**原文链接**: [https://www.activtrak.com/news/productivity-lab-ai-users-longer-workdays/](https://www.activtrak.com/news/productivity-lab-ai-users-longer-workdays/)

ActivTrak生产力实验室一项基于9000多名知识工作者实际数字工作模式的新研究，揭示了AI用户工作习惯的显著变化。研究表明，AI用户的工作日比非AI用户长达90分钟，平均工作时间为8.4至9.1小时，而非AI用户为7.6小时。

主要发现包括：
*   **更长的工作日：** AI用户工作时间更长。
*   **加班时间增加：** 重度AI用户每天额外增加18分钟的“第三班”下班后工作。
*   **更多应用切换：** 重度AI用户切换应用的频率比非AI用户高15.2%（每小时1.1次，非AI用户为每小时0.95次）。
*   **专注时间缩短：** 与轻度AI用户（48.2分钟）相比，重度AI用户的不间断专注时间缩短了22%（37.8分钟）。
*   **会议更少：** 重度AI用户花在正式会议上的时间更少（3% vs 5%）。

该研究还强调了AI访问方式的影响。嵌入式AI工具与较少的应用切换（每小时0.6次切换）相关，而基于浏览器的AI则带来了“转椅税”，导致应用切换显著增多（每小时4.2次）。这一点在不同职位中显而易见：使用嵌入式AI的软件工程师切换次数较少，而依赖基于浏览器的AI的运营经理则切换更频繁。

首席客户官Gabriela Mauch强调，尽管AI可以加快任务速度，但它目前导致工作日更长、上下文切换增加和专注度降低。她建议领导者优先关注AI整合带来的可衡量生产力提升和效率，而不仅仅是跟踪使用情况。

## 6. 物理可控的照片重照明

**原文标题**: Physically Controllable Relighting of Photographs

**原文链接**: [https://yaksoy.github.io/PhysicalRelighting/](https://yaksoy.github.io/PhysicalRelighting/)

由Careaga和Aksoy（SIGGRAPH 2025）发表的论文《照片的物理可控重照明》提出了一种开创性的方法，能够对真实世界照片进行重照明，实现对光源的明确、基于物理的控制，类似于Blender等3D计算机图形管线。

这种创新方法结合了中级计算机视觉、基于物理的渲染和神经渲染。它首先利用单目几何和固有成分估计，从一张照片中推断出场景的彩色网格表示。随后，用户可以定义所需的3D照明，包括聚光灯、点光源或环境光。之后，使用路径追踪引擎生成新照明下场景的近似渲染，并将其输入神经渲染器，从而产生最终的、逼真的重照明结果。

一个关键方面是其自监督训练方法，该方法采用可微分渲染，在原始的真实世界照片集合上训练神经渲染器，从而确保了强大的野外泛化能力。这种方法通过为现有照片中的光照操作提供前所未有的物理控制和逼真外观，显著推进了图像编辑领域，而这种能力以前仅限于合成场景。该工作正在申请专利，并借鉴了作者在固有图像分解和光照协调方面的先前研究。

## 9. Next-Level Benchmarking Overfitting – Thomas Neumann

**原文标题**: Next-Level Benchmarking Overfitting – Thomas Neumann

**原文链接**: [http://databasearchitects.blogspot.com/2026/11/next-level-benchmarking-overfitting.html](http://databasearchitects.blogspot.com/2026/11/next-level-benchmarking-overfitting.html)

Thomas Neumann's blog post, "Next-Level Benchmarking Overfitting," warns of a crisis in database benchmarking caused by AI agents over-optimizing for specific benchmarks, leading to unrealistic performance figures. He highlights the GenDB-V system, which claims 22x-61x speedups over engines like Umbra. Neumann argues these gains are infeasible and achieved through "insane tricks," such as extensive precomputation with query-specific data structures or hard-coding knowledge about the TPC-H data generator's row numbering. Although GenDB-V claims formal verification with a fallback to a slow generic path, this fallback is never triggered with official TPC-H data, rendering the optimizations practical only for the benchmark.

Jiale Lao, a GenDB-V author, largely confirms Neumann's observations. He clarifies that GenDB-V aims to build specialized engines for specific customer workloads (schema, data, hot query templates), not general-purpose ones, viewing it as complementary to systems like Umbra. Lao emphasizes that formal verification, ensuring correctness for *every* database of a schema, is GenDB-V's main contribution. He concedes that hard-coding `dbgen` specifics is unfair and will constrain future agent behavior. Lao proposes an "adversarial loop" for future benchmarks, where optimized engines face new, adversarial workloads to test true generalization, and invites the community to develop a shared formal semantics for SQL to advance verified database systems.

## 10. Huawei releases MACaQ, its internal quantizer for local inference

**原文标题**: Huawei releases MACaQ, its internal quantizer for local inference

**原文链接**: [https://github.com/huawei-csl/MACaQ](https://github.com/huawei-csl/MACaQ)

Huawei has released MACaQ, its internal quantizer designed for high-quality local inference across various AI models. MACaQ offers model-agnostic quantization, supporting not only text but also image, video, and audio models. It integrates with popular inference frameworks like llama.cpp, vLLM, Diffusers, and PyTorch.

A key advantage of MACaQ is its efficiency: it can quantize a 122-billion-parameter model in just 64 minutes using only 32.5 GB of device memory. It boasts superior performance, achieving lower perplexity than established quantizers like MR-GPTQ and DuQuant++, or matching their best results with significantly reduced quantization time (11x faster).

MACaQ supports multiple backends:
*   **Diffusers:** For image, video, and audio generation (W4A4).
*   **llama.cpp:** For 2/3/4-bit GGUF text model quantization, including MACaQ-R for refining existing GGUFs with standard llama.cpp compatibility.
*   **vLLM:** For text generation and serving (W4A4 or weight-only).
*   **PyTorch:** For speech generation (W4A4).
Additionally, W4A4 kernels for Ascend hardware are available through specific CANN pull requests.

Users are encouraged to star the project's repository. MACaQ is licensed under Apache License 2.0, and a research paper detailing its methodology is available for citation.

## 16. The hardest notes to find on the guitar

**原文标题**: The hardest notes to find on the guitar

**原文链接**: [https://www.gitori.com/blog/articles/hardest-notes-on-the-guitar-fretboard](https://www.gitori.com/blog/articles/hardest-notes-on-the-guitar-fretboard)

Based on data from Gitori's "Find the Notes" game, which collected 275,169 answers from over 10,000 players, a heatmap reveals the hardest and easiest notes to identify on a guitar fretboard.

**Summary of Findings:**

*   **Hardest Areas:** The upper middle of the neck is the most challenging. Specifically, the G string at frets 6 to 9 is the "red zone," with the B and D strings at frets 7 to 9 close behind. The single most difficult spot is the G string at fret 8 (E♭), where users are incorrect approximately one-third of the time.
*   **Easiest Areas:** The edges of the fretboard are the easiest to navigate. The low E string is the easiest overall, followed by the high E string. Open strings and notes at the 12th fret are missed only about 6% of the time, significantly less than the roughly 20% error rate around frets 8 and 9.
*   **Accidentals vs. Naturals:** Sharps and flats (accidentals) are generally harder to identify. About 16% of accidental notes are answered incorrectly, compared to 12% for natural notes. Additionally, accidentals take about 11% longer to find (2.5 seconds vs. 2.2 seconds). The hardest specific note *names* to identify are F♯, B♭, A♭, and E♭, while 'E' is the easiest.

## 23. A general purpose Apple Silicon layer for x86-64 PCs

**原文标题**: A general purpose Apple Silicon layer for x86-64 PCs

**原文链接**: [https://github.com/tylertms/Stela](https://github.com/tylertms/Stela)

Stela is an experimental project aiming to create a general-purpose Apple Silicon layer for x86-64 PCs, enabling macOS to boot and run on compatible hardware. It functions as a UEFI boot program, featuring an arm64-to-x86-64 translator, a custom Metal driver for NVIDIA GPUs, and a model of Apple Silicon hardware (specifically the Mac mini M2, running macOS 27).

Currently in very early development (v0.1.0), Stela is slow and has a low chance of successful booting, requiring significant testing and hardware support improvements. Its core components include an ahead-of-time and just-in-time compiler that translates over 3,000 AArch64 instruction encodings to x86-64, compatible with x86-64-v2 CPUs (2011 or newer). The NVIDIA driver supports GTX 16, RTX 20 series, and newer GPUs, compiling Metal's shader IR (AIR) directly to Mesa's IR (NIR).

Requirements for Stela include an x86-64-v2 CPU, UEFI with Secure Boot off, 8GB RAM, a supported NVIDIA GPU, an 8GB+ USB stick, a 60GB+ empty GPT partition, and network access during installation to download macOS. Installation involves running a setup utility which prepares the USB stick, from which the PC then boots.
