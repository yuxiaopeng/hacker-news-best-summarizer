## 6. Bleached corals thrive within days when touching healthy neighbour

**原文标题**: Bleached corals thrive within days when touching healthy neighbour

**原文链接**: [https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-03266-x](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-03266-x)

A new study offers hope for reversing coral bleaching, demonstrating that bleached coral fragments can rapidly recover their health and color when placed in direct contact with a healthy fragment from the same genetic individual.

Coral bleaching, caused by rising sea temperatures, involves corals expelling their symbiotic algae (Symbiodiniaceae), leading to starvation and death. Scientists Stephen Palumbi and Danny Collins at Stanford University and Hopkins Marine Station observed this accidental recovery and subsequently confirmed it through controlled experiments.

In their study, published in Science Advances, bleached fragments of *Pocillopora damicornis* touching healthy fragments from the same animal fully recovered within weeks, with symbiont populations doubling much faster than natural recovery rates. High-powered microscopy revealed that the fragments fuse via a tissue bridge, enabling the transfer of the vital algae. Crucially, this "healing touch" only worked for corals of identical genetic makeup (a genet); fragments from different species or even different individuals of the same species did not fuse or facilitate recovery.

This discovery that full and fast recovery is possible within days through contact provides an inspiring new direction for developing scalable solutions to combat widespread coral decline globally.

**原文标题**: DeepSeek search and fetch – $0.75 per 1000 successful requests

**原文链接**: [https://api-docs.deepseek.com/guides/search/](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/guides/search/)

DeepSeek提供搜索和抓取API，使代理和RAG应用能够访问实时网络内容。它们都是原始JSON API，需要通过 `https://api.deepseek.com` 使用bearer token进行认证。

**搜索API** (`/v1/tools/search`) 利用DeepSeek自有的搜索引擎根据给定查询查找网页，并返回结构化结果。关键请求字段包括 `query`、`count`（1-50个结果）和 `need_content`（用于抓取页面内容）。它支持 `site:`、`lang:`、`after:` 等高级查询操作符，以及精确短语匹配。响应提供 `title`、`url`、`snippet`、`summary`（供模型使用）、`content`（如果请求）、`rerank_score` 和 `authority`。

**抓取API** (`/v1/tools/fetch`) 根据其 `url` 检索单个网页，以Markdown文本形式返回其内容以及页面 `title`。

这两种API的**计费**标准是每1000次*成功*请求0.75美元。只有当请求返回HTTP 200状态码并产生非空结果（搜索数组或抓取内容）时，才会被计费。失败的请求或空结果不收费。通过DeepSeek Harness、Claude Code或Codex进行的DeepSeek API调用目前免费，直至2026年11月1日。

**速率限制**设置为，搜索API和抓取API分别在账户层面独立限制为每秒5次请求（QPS）。抓取API还强制执行每个域名的QPS限制。错误采用与OpenAI兼容的格式，并通过特定的HTTP状态码提供关于无效请求、余额不足或速率限制等问题的详细信息。

## 9. 纽约宣布游戏所有权法案，要求所有权而非授权

**原文标题**: NY announces game ownership bill requiring ownership rather than licensing

**原文链接**: [https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/new-york-rep-announces-game-ownership-bill-that-would-require-consumer-ownership-rather-than-sale-of-licenses-would-also-require-publishers-to-keep-the-game-functional-after-support-ends](https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/new-york-rep-announces-game-ownership-bill-that-would-require-consumer-ownership-rather-than-sale-of-licenses-would-also-require-publishers-to-keep-the-game-functional-after-support-ends)

New York Rep. Ritchie Torres has introduced the "Power To The Players Act," legislation aiming to redefine video game purchases as consumer ownership rather than a revocable license. This bill directs the FTC to create regulations combating anti-consumer practices, specifically mandating that games provide an offline mode before their online servers are shut down.

This initiative responds to widespread player frustration over games becoming unplayable after publishers discontinue server support, exemplified by Ubisoft's *The Crew* and the impending shutdown of *NBA 2K25*. Publishers like Sony have even legally argued that clicking "purchase" does not grant durable rights, only a temporary, revocable license.

Similar legislation has been proposed in Pennsylvania, and the "Stop Killing Games" movement advocates for publishers to ensure games remain functional post-discontinuation through offline modes or community servers. The Power To The Players Act is a significant step towards securing consumer rights in the gaming industry, challenging the current model where purchased games can effectively have an expiration date. Its effectiveness will depend on clearly defining ownership and associated rights.
