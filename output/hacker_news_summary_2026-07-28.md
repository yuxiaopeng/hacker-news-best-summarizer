## 3. 我们对开放权重模型的立场

**原文标题**: Our position on open-weights models

**原文链接**: [https://www.anthropic.com/news/position-open-weights-models](https://www.anthropic.com/news/position-open-weights-models)

Anthropic首席执行官达里奥·阿莫迪（Dario Amodei）澄清了该公司在开放权重模型上的立场，明确否认支持任何禁令，尽管近期出现了一些讨论和指责。他表示，不具备危险能力的开放权重模型是一种公共产品，而保护主义禁令并不能解决他主要的国家安全担忧。

阿莫迪概述了两大担忧：
1.  威权政府（例如中国）开发出比美国更强大的AI，从而导致军事优势或深度压迫。这种担忧与模型是否为开放权重无关。
2.  强大AI被滥用于网络或生物攻击，或出现重大的对齐问题。尽管开放权重模型*可能*带来更高的风险，因为难以实施防护措施或监控其使用，但禁止合法的美国企业使用它们并不能阻止不良行为者。

相反，Anthropic始终主张采取三项措施：
1.  限制向中国销售强大的芯片和芯片制造设备并打击走私，以防止中国训练出更高级的模型。
2.  打击工业规模的“蒸馏”操作，这种操作使中国能够更高效地开发高级模型并规避芯片禁令。
3.  对*所有*具备足够能力的模型（无论是开放还是封闭的）实施强制性安全测试，在发布前评估其网络、生物和对齐风险。

尽管同意一封公开信中关于开放权重模型能够扩大访问和竞争的观点，但阿莫迪不同意它们必然会改善安全防护，或普遍上对防御者比攻击者更有利，尤其是在生物威胁方面。Anthropic的重点仍然是定向干预措施：芯片管制、打击“蒸馏”以及普遍的安全测试，而非一概禁止开放权重模型。

## 7. Bun的Rust重写进展如何？

**原文标题**: How is the Bun rewrite in Rust going?

**原文链接**: [https://lockwood.dev/ai/2026/07/27/how-is-the-bun-rewrite-in-rust-going.html](https://lockwood.dev/ai/2026/07/27/how-is-the-bun-rewrite-in-rust-going.html)

The author expresses skepticism about claims supporting high company valuations based on AI's coding capabilities, using the Bun rewrite in Rust as a case study. Jarred Sumner of Bun (acquired by Anthropic) claimed the rewrite was completed by Anthropic's AI in 11 days (May 3-14, 2026) for $165,000 in API calls, then merged to main.

However, the author's investigation as of July 27, 2026, raises doubts:
*   Six weeks post-merge, no new Bun release tag exists, an unusually long delay.
*   Open pull requests from "robobun" (Anthropic's Claude AI) surged from 1277 to 2475 in just over two weeks, implying continuous merging for 86 days would be needed.
*   The author argues the initial $165k likely omits significant CI/CD costs and ongoing Anthropic employee involvement. Analysis suggests the project continues to consume Claude credits and direct Anthropic employee effort, even working weekends now.
*   The true cost is estimated to be nearing $800k, far exceeding the initial figure, and the rewrite appears far from "done."

The article questions whether the rewrite was truly "worth the money," linking this project to a broader critique of the current AI hype cycle and the "valuation money riding on the coding capabilities of AI." The author notes other AI-driven projects, like Anthropic's C compiler and Cursor's FastRender, have also seen no recent commits.
